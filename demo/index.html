It comes to rest.

Stop scrolling near a card and it glides to that stop. It never pulls on the rest of your page.

  • Half a second, then a glide
  • Glide, reach and snap
  • Glides through Lenis

Reduced motion gets stills.

If a visitor's device asks for less motion, the flight becomes one still per screen, and the page scrolls as usual.

Show stills

Phones get their own cut.

On a tall screen the picture is cropped around a point you choose for each stop, so the subject stays in view.
